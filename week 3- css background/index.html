Chocolate Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Butter for frying

Cooking Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder and baking powder.
  2. Add eggs and milk into the bowl.
  3. Whisk everything until smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and add butter.
  5. Pour small portions of batter into the pan.
  6. Cook each pancake for 2 minutes on each side.
  7. Serve with chocolate or berries.
